Privacy

CleanYourVizio collects nothing. The pages contain no analytics, cookies or trackers, and nothing you do is sent to the author.

The TV page loads Google's Cast receiver library and the Outfit font from Google Fonts, which receive the usual request information (such as your IP address) under Google's own policies. App artwork is loaded from Vizio's public image server. Weather and app tiles are sent to the TV only by a controller you run on your own home network.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/theferg42/cleanyourvizio.
